# Imagegen reference assets

The three reference images were created with the built-in `image_gen` tool and then copied byte-identically to both the experiment folder and `runtime/4090/input`.

## Asset 1: environment

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an empty near-future Tokyo rooftop greenhouse and observation deck after rain, with a glass greenhouse, planter boxes, metal railing, distant city skyline, wet reflective concrete, cool blue-hour light, and subtle warm practical lights. Cinematic 16:9 landscape, no people, no animals, no logos, no readable text, no watermark.

## Asset 2: identity portrait

Create a fictional Japanese woman in her late 20s with a short dark-brown bob, silver hair clip above the right temple, tiny crescent-shaped scar on the left eyebrow, warm brown eyes, mustard-yellow raincoat, and slate-blue sweater. Place her in the same rooftop greenhouse at blue hour. Medium three-quarter portrait, realistic live-action photography, no text or watermark.

## Asset 3: identity/profile reference

Using Asset 2 as the identity reference, preserve the same woman, hair, clip, eyebrow scar, eyes, raincoat, sweater, and greenhouse. Change only the pose and framing to a wider three-quarter full-body profile beside the wet rooftop railing. Keep the same blue-hour photographic style and no text or watermark.

## Video prompt

`<Picture 1>` is the empty near-future Tokyo rooftop greenhouse and observation deck environment. `<Picture 2>` is Aoi, the same fictional woman shown in the portrait reference. `<Picture 3>` is Aoi shown full-body in profile. At blue hour after rain, Aoi walks slowly out of the greenhouse onto the wet rooftop deck, pauses beside the railing, and looks over the city. The camera makes a slow cinematic dolly from a wide establishing view toward her, with gentle natural movement in her short dark-brown bob and yellow raincoat. Preserve her exact face, silver hair clip, tiny crescent-shaped scar on her left eyebrow, warm brown eyes, mustard-yellow raincoat, slate-blue sweater, and the greenhouse architecture. Realistic live-action motion, calm serious mood, no dialogue, no text, no subtitles.
