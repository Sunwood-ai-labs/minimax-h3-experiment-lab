# Brief

Use the five-segment MiniMax-H3 Motion Context output as the photographic MV plate.
Keep the H3-generated audio as the music spine and add readable Japanese lyric motion
on top without replacing the generated footage.

The visual treatment is dark, cinematic and editorial: one orange accent, large lyric
type, small monospace song metadata, a restrained lower-third panel, and subtle
audio-reactive movement driven by pre-extracted frequency bands. The lyric lines are
the planned text supplied to the H3 prompts; the experiment must distinguish planned
lyrics from independently verified vocal transcription.
